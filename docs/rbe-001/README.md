## Constitutional principles

- The Review Board has no interest in whether a proposal succeeds or fails. Its sole responsibility is to determine whether the conclusion presented is justified by the evidence, reasoning, and methodology.
- The burden of justification rests with the conclusion, not with its critics.
- Every decision shall be reproducible.

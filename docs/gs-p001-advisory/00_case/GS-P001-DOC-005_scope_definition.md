# GS-P001-DOC-005 — Scope Definition

**Document version:** 1.0.0  
**Study:** GS-P001  
**Execution mode:** `ADVISORY_DRY_RUN`

## Included scope

GS-P001 includes evidence concerning:

- United States residential property management;
- maintenance-request intake and acknowledgement;
- resident-to-manager maintenance communication;
- manager-to-contractor or maintenance-team handoffs;
- status updates, scheduling, access coordination and closure communication;
- missing, delayed, duplicated or incomplete maintenance information;
- operational burden on residents, property managers, coordinators, contractors and owners;
- residential property-management software where the evidence relates directly to maintenance communication workflows;
- government, regulator, court, consumer, industry and software-review evidence that satisfies admission rules.

## Excluded scope

GS-P001 excludes:

- commercial-only, industrial or non-residential facilities management;
- construction-project management;
- accounting, rent collection, leasing or HOA governance unless directly tied to maintenance communication;
- building-condition evidence with no communication or workflow dimension;
- product feature comparisons or vendor marketing claims;
- general dissatisfaction that cannot be tied to an operational maintenance-communication failure;
- evidence outside the United States;
- synthetic, demo, fabricated, unattributed or unverifiable records;
- software design, implementation, deployment, pricing or investment decisions.

## Unit of analysis

The primary unit of analysis is a distinct, traceable evidence item describing or measuring an operational maintenance-communication failure, its consequences, or the workflow burden created by it.

Multiple copies, reposts or summaries of the same underlying incident must not be counted as independent evidence.

## Geographic boundary

United States only. National, state and local evidence may be admitted when the jurisdiction and relevance are recorded.

## Time boundary

Current evidence is preferred. Historical evidence may be admitted only when the underlying workflow remains relevant and that continuing relevance is documented.

## Population boundary

Relevant actors include:

- residents and tenants;
- residential property managers and management firms;
- maintenance coordinators and internal maintenance staff;
- third-party contractors and vendors;
- residential property owners where their evidence concerns maintenance communication operations.

## Decision boundary

The advisory review may conclude only whether the evidence justifies progression into reusable component definition. It cannot approve construction, funding, procurement, production deployment or publication of binding claims.

## Deferred questions

The following are deferred to later work if progression is justified:

- exact component boundaries;
- target customer segment prioritization;
- revenue model and pricing;
- technical architecture;
- vendor selection;
- production controls and service-level commitments.

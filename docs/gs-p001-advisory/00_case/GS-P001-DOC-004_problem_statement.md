# GS-P001-DOC-004 — Problem Statement

**Document version:** 1.0.0  
**Study:** GS-P001  
**Execution mode:** `ADVISORY_DRY_RUN`

## Problem to be tested

Residential property maintenance communication in the United States may contain recurring operational failures caused by fragmented communication, delayed responses, incomplete information, poor status visibility, handoff failures and inconsistent workflows across residents, property managers, maintenance coordinators, contractors and property owners.

The study must determine whether those failures are supported by enough verified, independent and commercially relevant evidence to justify progressing from research into the definition of a reusable software component.

## Research question

> Is there enough verified, independent operational pain in U.S. residential property management maintenance communication to justify building a reusable software component?

## Decision boundary

The review may recommend progression into component definition only. It may not authorize software construction, funding, procurement, production deployment or commercial claims.

## Evidence standard

The problem is considered demonstrated only when admitted evidence is:

- independently sourced;
- verifiable;
- within the defined scope;
- traceable to preserved source records;
- sufficiently repeated to indicate more than isolated incidents;
- commercially relevant to a reusable operational workflow;
- free from synthetic, demo or fabricated records.

## Positive finding

A positive finding requires evidence that the pain is recurring, operational rather than merely subjective, independently observable, traceable and relevant to a repeatable software capability.

## Insufficient finding

The evidence is insufficient when it is too weak, too dependent on a single source, not verifiable, not current enough to support present operations, outside scope, materially duplicated, or unable to support a commercially relevant conclusion.

## Procedural failure

The review is procedurally incomplete when required governance records, provenance, reviewer eligibility, conflict declarations, quorum or audit integrity are missing. A procedurally incomplete outcome must not be converted into a positive decision.

## Neutrality requirement

GS-P001 is designed to test the evidence, not defend a preferred product idea. A negative or insufficient-evidence result is valid and must be preserved without override.

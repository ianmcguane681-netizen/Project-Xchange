# GS-P001-DOC-002 — Study Definition

**Document version:** 1.0.0  
**Study:** GS-P001  
**Execution mode:** `ADVISORY_DRY_RUN`  
**Authority status:** Non-binding

## Purpose

GS-P001 is a standalone proof system designed to determine whether verified, independent operational pain in U.S. residential property management maintenance communication is sufficient to justify defining a reusable software component.

GS-P001 is not a product demo, a software prototype, a revenue forecast, or a production deployment. Its purpose is to test whether the Provena evidence and governance methodology can produce a traceable, repeatable, commercially relevant and auditable conclusion.

## Research question

> Is there enough verified, independent operational pain in U.S. residential property management maintenance communication to justify building a reusable software component?

## Hypotheses

**Primary hypothesis:** Verified evidence demonstrates recurring, independent and commercially meaningful operational pain sufficient to justify progression into component definition.

**Null hypothesis:** The admitted evidence is insufficient in quality, independence, repeatability, traceability or commercial relevance to justify progression.

## Primary objective

Evaluate whether the admissible evidence supports progression from research into a bounded component-definition phase.

## Secondary objectives

- Prove that GS-P001 evidence can be admitted without fabricated, synthetic or unverifiable records.
- Demonstrate end-to-end traceability from finding to source.
- Demonstrate deterministic review execution and reproducible output.
- Validate separation between advisory outcome, human ratification, publication and implementation authority.
- Produce an independently verifiable audit bundle.

## Required deliverables

- Phase A governance package.
- Verified evidence and source registers.
- Claim-to-evidence traceability mapping.
- Reviewer declarations and reports supplied only by eligible human reviewers.
- Deterministic advisory outcome.
- JSON and Markdown audit bundle with checksums.

## Success definition

The study is successful when the process reaches a procedurally valid and reproducible outcome, regardless of whether that outcome is positive, conditional, insufficient-evidence, or negative.

Success requires:

- admitted evidence is verified and within scope;
- every material finding traces to admitted evidence;
- reviewer independence and conflicts are recorded;
- no human action is fabricated or simulated;
- the same inputs and rules reproduce the same advisory outcome;
- exported artifacts pass integrity verification;
- all outputs remain explicitly non-binding.

## Non-goals

GS-P001 does not:

- build or approve software;
- authorize investment or procurement;
- select a technology stack;
- estimate revenue or market share;
- validate a specific vendor or product;
- activate RBM-001 as binding authority;
- create Provena Foundry features, dashboards or production services;
- substitute AI or automation for human review seats, signatures, ratification or publication authority.

## Separation of products

GS-P001 and Provena Foundry are separate products. GS-P001 is the standalone proof system. Provena Foundry is a future operating environment and is not part of this advisory execution.
